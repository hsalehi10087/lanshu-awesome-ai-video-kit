# Wink-and-clap image-to-video prompt

Start frame: `start-frame.jpg` (720x1280, 9:16)

Suggested models: Kling 3 / Veo 3.1 / Wan 2.2 I2V, 5 seconds.

## Prompt (EN)
The man in the dark suit slowly turns his head toward the camera and looks straight into the lens.
He gives a playful wink with his left eye and a slight smile, then raises both hands to chest
height and claps several times. The tweet box, the logo and the red Persian headline stay
completely still and unchanged. Static camera, natural and realistic motion, consistent face.

## Negative prompt
warped text, changing text, extra fingers, deformed hands, face morphing, camera shake, blur
